ContextVault

Type /context your question in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and press Enter.

Off by default. Injecting uploads excerpts of your local archive to whichever site you are on, so the default is to let you read it first and press Enter yourself.

Your conversations stay on your machine until you run the command. The extension only talks to the address above — never to any other server — and only when you type /context.
